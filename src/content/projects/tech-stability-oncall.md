## Problem

A fast-growing domain accumulates operational drag: slow deploys, blind spots in
monitoring, recurring errors, and AWS spend that creeps up unnoticed. On top of
that, incidents need someone who can actually drive them to resolution — not just
acknowledge the page.

## Approach

I led and implemented a run of stability initiatives, each targeting a specific
weak point:

- **Deployment time reduction** — shorter, safer release cycles.
- **Improved monitoring and alerting** — so problems surface before customers
  feel them.
- **Error reduction** and **latency improvement** across the stack.
- **Automation suites** to take manual, error-prone steps out of the loop.
- **AWS cost reduction** — trimming spend without sacrificing reliability.

On **on-call**, I served as the backbone of the team: investigating issues,
driving root-cause analysis, owning merchant communication during incidents, and
turning one-off fires into long-term fixes.

## Outcome

Tech stability and quality measurably improved across the Returns and Prep
domains — faster to ship, quicker to detect, cheaper to run — and incidents were
handled with real ownership rather than triage-and-forget.
